Getting here
We're right in the historic center, a short walk from the Cathedral and the San Pablo Bridge, next to the Hanging Houses. The streets in this area are cobbled and somewhat steep — the easiest way is on foot from the upper old town.
Parking
If you're driving, the nearest car park is about 187 metres away (Cintra). Street parking is limited in this pedestrian area.

The taberna

The taste of Cuenca, with the fiesta of San Mateo at the bar

Right in the old town of Cuenca, Taberna Albero serves traditional Castilian-La Mancha and Mediterranean cuisine in a small space full of character: a local wine cellar, bullfighting décor, and a San Mateo ceramic figure presiding over the bar that leaves no doubt where you are.

The menu looks after everyone at the table, with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options alongside the classics — morteruelo, ajoarriero, zarajos — made the way they've always been made.
